01 / The series

What is RESIDUE.

RESIDUE. is an independent docu-series about growing up in Fort Wayne, Indiana.

Young people from here sit down and tell it in their own words. The block, the school, the parties, the fights.

Eight short episodes on what we had to survive on our own.

And what it all leaves behind.
